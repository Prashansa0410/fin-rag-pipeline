# Financial Operations Handbook
Version 4.0

Terminology:
- T+1: Settlement occurs one business day after trade.
- Cutoff time: The time by which transactions must be submitted for same-day processing (default 18:00 UTC).
- Reconciliation: The process of matching internal records against external partner logs.

Escalation Rules:
Any transaction exceeding $10,000 without complete beneficial owner information must be flagged for High Risk compliance review.
